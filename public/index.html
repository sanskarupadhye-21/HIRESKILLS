Telemetry 01

Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How does speech telemetry work in-browser?

HireSkill AI leverages the native HTML5 Web Speech API to transcribe answers with microsecond timestamps, allowing client-side algorithms to evaluate filler frequencies and pace.

Is my camera or microphone recording saved?

No. Video frames and raw audio are processed purely in ephemeral RAM within your browser. Only final aggregated numeric scores and question responses are saved to your dashboard.

What roles can I practice for?

We provide specialized tracks for Software Engineering, Cloud Architecture, Python & Data Science, Frontend Systems, and Engineering Leadership.
